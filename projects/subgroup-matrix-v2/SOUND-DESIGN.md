# Closing the Gap — Sound direction

Status: authored cue sheet; V2 audio has not been generated or auditioned.
Azure Speech only for online narration. No Google narration, licensed stock song,
paid video platform, or artist imitation is part of this plan.

## Voice

Azure `en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural`, close and measured, an engineer guiding a
peer through an important result. Confidence comes from clarity. Do not perform a
trailer voice or read every sentence with identical cadence. Preserve sentence
rests, especially “First, the kernel.” and “Then, the model.”

Keep literal screenplay text in project.json. Screen text retains exact product
spellings while spoken text expands difficult numbers. At audio production,
check LinAlg (lin-alg), Vulkan, SIMD, WebGPU, Dawn, ONNX, XMX, FMA, MLP, Phi-4-mini,
and both Radeon names against normal playback. Test SSML pronunciation only if
necessary; do not silently change factual screen labels to match an ASR guess.

Word counts are estimates, not subtitle timings. Synthesize sentence WAVs, measure
durations, then place captions. Aim for 0.7 s front space and at least 1 s tail space
per scene, with longer held evidence intervals. If a line does not fit, edit or
redistribute the scene before resorting to moderate stretching; never exceed 1.3×
or cut the last word.

## Original score

Compose a restrained electronic chamber score, approximately 84 BPM, 4/4. Use an
unfinished two-note motif (D–A as a starting sketch), warm sustained harmonics,
a rounded bass foundation, sparse felt-like key transients and understated
percussion. Change orchestration and harmonic tension with the narrative; avoid
continuous identical arpeggiation, synthetic fanfares and huge impact booms.

| Time | Arrangement and story function |
|---|---|
| 00:00–00:12 | Soft room texture, then isolated two-note motif. Voice enters before a beat establishes. |
| 00:12–00:40 | Subtle pulse enters with matrix work; bass joins hardware. Keep spectral space around voice. |
| 00:40–01:16 | Withdraw layers at the D3D gap; brighter suspended harmony at LinAlg, then restraint at “usable?”. |
| 01:16–01:46 | Add restrained percussion through the fixes; movement feels earned. Remove percussion approaching measurement. |
| 01:46–02:36 | Air and low sustained harmony. A brief musical dip before each evidence reveal; clarity over excitement. No climax that suggests universal speedup. |
| 02:36–02:48 | Resolve the motif as the request returns to the device; let the final chord decay into room tone. |

This arrangement is not yet implemented by the existing minimal looping score.
Before the sample stage, implement reusable section-level music control or deliver
a locally composed score stem, with per-project selection, caching and provenance.
Do not mark this requirement complete merely because music is enabled.

## Sound effects

Tie effects to contact or consequence: one dry tile click at cooperative contact,
a quiet pass through a corrected gate, one low-gain confirmation at a settled
measurement. Most text entrances remain silent. No tick on every letter or bar,
no repeated transition whoosh. Left-to-right placement may lightly follow the
request; keep narration centered and mono-compatible.

## Mix and QA

Use smooth voice-driven ducking, retain quiet gaps, and avoid per-sample threshold
pumping. Keep speech intelligible on laptop speakers as well as headphones.
Target a two-pass normalized mix near −14 LUFS and ≤ −1.5 dBTP, then measure the
encoded AAC, inspect audio/video sync and scene-boundary tails, and listen at
normal speed. Produce editable captions from measured speech clips.

Record automatic transcription, numerical pronunciation review, motion timing,
and actual listening as separate checks. None has yet been performed for V2.
