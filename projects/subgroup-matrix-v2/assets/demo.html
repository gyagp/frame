Why subgroup-matrix hardware matters

One subgroup. Two matrix execution paths.

A subgroup is one hardware-sized team of lanes (a warp or wave). For inference, matrix multiplies dominate the compute in attention and MLP layers; subgroup-matrix hardware increases token throughput and reduces latency.
